About Me

I am a Research Fellow at Microsoft Research (M365 Research), focusing on structured representations of LLMs and AI agent reliability. I graduated with a B.Tech in Computer Science from IIT Kanpur. Previously, I conducted research on AI for medical imaging at the IoT Lab, IIT Kanpur, and worked on diffusion models at the CV Lab, EPFL in Switzerland.

Beyond research, I am deeply engaged in the startup and venture capital space. I served as an Investment Associate at HP Tech Ventures, evaluating AI startups for strategic investment. I actively participate in hackathons and enjoy writing about the intersection of technology, product-market fit, and investing.
