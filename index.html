About Me

I am a Research Fellow at Microsoft Research (M365 Research) where I work with Anson Bastos, Mayukh Das, and Chetan Bansal on structured representations of LLMs and AI agent reliability. Prior to MSR, I collaborated with Dr. Priyanka Bagade at the IoT Lab, IIT Kanpur on AI for medical imaging, and earlier worked with Dr. Ashutosh Modi on Indian Sign Language Recognition (EMNLP ’22).

Outside research, I love the world of startups—hackathons, pitching events, and weekend technology builds keep me energized. I also spend time in the investing/VC space, advising on product-market fit and following the Gartner Hype Cycle more than the “AI bubble vs. no bubble” debate. Selected Publications

  1. Indian Sign Language Recognition using Graph Neural Networks
    Shashwat Gupta, Ashutosh Modi
    The 2022 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP), 2022
    [Abs] [Bib] [PDF] [Code]

    Abstract: Sign language recognition is a challenging task due to the complex spatial and temporal dependencies in sign gestures. In this paper, we propose a novel Graph Neural Network (GNN) based approach for Indian Sign Language (ISL) recognition. We construct a graph representation of the hand skeleton and use GNNs to capture the spatial relationships between joints. We also incorporate temporal information using a temporal convolution network. Our method achieves state-of-the-art results on a large-scale ISL dataset.

  2. AI for Medical Imaging in IoT Contexts
    Shashwat Gupta, Priyanka Bagade
    IoT Lab, IIT Kanpur, Research
    [Abs] [PDF]

    Abstract: This research explores the application of Artificial Intelligence techniques for medical imaging within Internet of Things (IoT) environments. We investigate lightweight deep learning models suitable for edge devices to analyze medical images for rapid diagnosis. The study focuses on optimizing model performance while maintaining high accuracy under resource-constrained conditions typical of IoT deployments.
