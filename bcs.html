Brain and Cognitive Society (BCS)

Exploring the Intersection of AI and Neuroscience

During my time at IIT Kanpur, I served as a Leader at the Brain and Cognitive Society (BCS). BCS is a student-led organization that focuses on bridging the gap between machine learning and neuroscience. Our aim is to reverse-engineer human intelligence by studying computational cognition, ultimately to help build more general and intelligent AI systems for the future.

As a Leader, my role was to foster a community of curious students—ranging from freshmen to seniors—and guide them through the complex yet fascinating world of cognitive sciences. This involved organizing technical workshops and hands-on projects, giving students the platform to understand not just the code behind a neural network, but the biological inspiration driving it. Our projects ranged from analyzing EEG data and eye-tracking studies to developing practical tech solutions like specialized chatbots for local law enforcement.

A major milestone during my tenure was helping spearhead Neural Net November, the society's flagship three-day event. We hosted hackathons, interactive workshops, and invited talks from notable alumni and researchers. Leading this initiative greatly honed my organizational skills and deepened my appreciation for interdisciplinary research, cementing my belief that the next big leap in artificial intelligence will deeply rely on understanding the biological brain.
