Welcome to my Den of Reflections

Welcome to the world of my reflections, where I discuss books, lifestyle, technology, and startups/investing.

Hello, I’m Shashwat (Shawn) Gupta, a Research-Fellow at Microsoft Research with a deep passion for technology and investment. Prior to Microsoft I was a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from IIT Kanpur in Computer Science (IITK CSE’24). My journey at IIT Kanpur has been a transformative experience, blending rigorous academics with hands-on projects in fields like machine learning and human-computer interaction.

Beyond the classroom, I have actively engaged in entrepreneurial ventures, founding startups and exploring the intricacies of venture capital. Through this blog, I aim to share my insights, experiences, and the valuable lessons I’ve learned along the way, with a focus on the intersection of technology and investment. Welcome to my blog, where I hope to inspire and inform fellow enthusiasts and aspiring professionals.

Why I Write, and Why This Blog?

I write to guide someone in my shoes or who wants to achieve something that I got at some point in my life. Think of it as a note to my younger self, someone who might be navigating similar challenges and aspirations elsewhere. Documenting and reflecting on my journey allows me to consolidate the lessons I’ve learned, providing a clearer perspective on my experiences and growth. Additionally, this blog serves as a platform for discussion and exchange of ideas related to technology, IIT, investing, and life in general. By sharing my insights, opening up conversations, I hope to connect with like-minded individuals who share my passions and curiosity.
