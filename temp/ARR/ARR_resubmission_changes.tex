\section{Revisions based on review qsVF}

\noindent 1) Quantitative statistics on states and actions:

Revision: We report quantitative statistics on states and actions in section 6.5 and in Appendix in section A.5.2 (figure 6) and in figures 21-28.

\noindent 2) Contextual Bandit:

Revision: We clarify that the single agent setup is a case of contextual bandit in section 3 and provide further justification of why the MDP setting is needed in Appendix section A.1.

\noindent 3) Qualitative analysis for helpfulness of representations:

Revision: We provide example case studies for qualitative analysis of usefulness of representations. To help with understanding we further highlight portions of the agentic trajectory that show the benefit of specific representations.
